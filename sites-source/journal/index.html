Journal settings & calculation definitions

Targets are your review benchmarks, not proof of an edge. Expectancy is average net P&L ÷ original planned dollar risk. At least 50 closed trades is a review milestone, not a guarantee. Profit factor uses positive gross P&L ÷ absolute negative gross P&L (before fees). Average win / loss and win rate use net results, with breakeven included in the win-rate denominator.

Drawdown is the largest realized peak-to-trough decline. Percentage drawdown needs a starting balance and uses the equity peak as its denominator. Balance means equity before the first recorded trade; no cash flows are modeled. Adherence requires every applicable checklist item marked Done and “All plan rules followed” marked Yes. Missing assessments count as not confirmed, not as passes.

Session names are user-selected. Hour and day groups use the reporting timezone. Period filters use entry date; equity includes earlier realized results in the opening balance. Screenshots export as filenames in CSV; use Full backup for image data and unfinished drafts. Dashboard reference ↗
